Kannada editor and Nudi to Unicode converter

Nudi to Unicode conversion turns Kannada text typed in the old Nudi or Baraha fonts into standard Unicode Kannada, which phones, websites and search engines can read. This editor does it in your browser, marks every glyph it could not convert, and uploads nothing.

Paste Nudi or Baraha text, open a TXT, Word or HTML file, or start typing.

  1. Pasted Nudi and Baraha text turns into Unicode by itself.
  2. Red marks a glyph that could not be converted. Amber marks a broken syllable. Select one to fix it.
  3. Choose Baraha letters above to type ಕನ್ನಡ as kannaDa.

    How to convert Nudi to Unicode

    1. Open your file with Open, or copy the text from Word and paste it into the sheet. Word files keep their fonts, so only the text set in Nudi or Baraha fonts is converted.
    2. Check the marks. Red is a glyph with no Kannada letter. Amber is a sign that cannot form a syllable. Select a mark, or press F8, and pick a fix.
    3. Choose Save and pick Word, plain text or web page.

    Questions

    Is my text uploaded?

    No. The converter is compiled to WebAssembly and runs on your device. The page loads its own files and makes no other network requests. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. Your last document is kept in this browser's storage so it is still here when you come back.

    Which fonts does it convert?

    Nudi fonts up to version 5, and Baraha ANSI fonts such as BRH Kannada. Text that already shows as Kannada letters is Unicode and needs no conversion.

    Can it convert Unicode to Nudi?

    Not yet. A Unicode to Nudi converter is coming.

    How do I type Kannada with an English keyboard?

    Choose Baraha letters and type words the way they sound: kannaDa gives ಕನ್ನಡ, nADu gives ನಾಡು. Capital letters give long vowels and retroflex sounds. Press Esc to keep a word in English letters.

    What do Normalize and Modernize do?

    Normalize joins vowel signs typed in 2 parts and removes doubled marks, so text that looks the same also searches the same. Modernize changes the Old Kannada letters ೞ ಱ ೝ to ಳ ರ ನ್.

    How accurate is it?

    Every glyph it cannot place is marked, and every syllable that is not valid Kannada is marked. A measured error rate on a checked set of real documents will be published when that test set is ready.

    Who made this?

    It is built on kannadi, an open-source Rust library for Kannada text by Prasad Subrahmanya, released under the MIT license. The fonts are Noto Sans Kannada and Noto Serif Kannada, under the SIL Open Font License.

    If kannadi helps your project, a mention or a link to prasad.tech is appreciated.
